# Mallside v16: fuller stores

The six product stations remain the anchors for paging and department selection. New physical stock fixtures add six wall bays, up to two double-sided floor rails/islands and a low front merchandise table, subject to fixture clearance. Outer product stations now leave usable side aisles. All fixture approaches, fitting-room entrances, checkout, returns and the mall exit share the tested navigation model. Stock photos and product actions use this branch's own active catalog only. Decorative garment silhouettes and packaged-stock shapes are original game art, not scans or additional product variants.

Repeated garments, hangers and folded stacks use instanced geometry to limit draw calls. Shelves have tiered stock and photographed product headers. Apparel displays have denser hanging stock and lower folded shelves. The front table uses an actual clothing model for folded apparel, bags for leather-goods stores, or photographed display models for other categories. Selecting a physical display walks to its clear approach and opens the referenced real product.

Two fitting cubicles have mirrors, benches, partitions and occupancy-controlled curtains. Styles without clothing use lower open consultation/style counters. Checkout and returns have staff, display registers, card readers, scanners, receipt rolls and bags. Their player interaction points now sit in front of the counters. The three waiting positions are distinct from the register and remain clear of the mall exit.

Eight desktop customers or five narrow-screen customers follow a route-based visit: browse, compare, try/test, queue, pay, leave and enter again. Rooms and the register have exclusive reservations, the payment queue advances in order, and customers give way to the player. Poses show browsing, fitting and payment; completed customers carry a shopping bag. The lifecycle pauses with the game, a modal, hidden document or player action. Reduced-motion mode retains static customer poses, and disabling crowds removes ambient customers while keeping the service staff. Nonwearable assortments use demos instead of changing clothes.

Customer visits are scenery simulation, independent of the player economy and merchant stock. No unverifiable listings, guest products, new prices or purchases are added. Pending catalogs remain marked and have no invented buyers. Brand palettes and researched signature interiors from v15 are retained; these remain original game interpretations rather than exact replicas of branches.

The interior toolbar sits in a separate row above the canvas. It no longer obscures back-wall merchandise or fitting rooms. On smaller screens it stacks while retaining the five primary actions. The initial interior camera is pulled back to show the fuller floor, and zoom/rotation remain available. Repeated photos now preserve their aspect ratio even when sharing an already loaded texture.
