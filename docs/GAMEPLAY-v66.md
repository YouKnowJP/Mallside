# Mallside v66 shopping edit

Open **Browse** in the game menu to search the catalog across all ten malls. Store shelves have the same size, price, and availability filters.

- **My saved size** uses the usual sizes from Player & options. Choose an exact size or format for a specific search, such as 128GB or a 37 mm case.
- **Price limits** include simulated sales tax and current game deals. **In stock** checks the exact size or format you selected.
- **On my wishlist** shows saved listings. **New this update** brings the new Apple and TUDOR shelf additions into view.
- **All shop listings** shows every matching branch and its mall. **One listing per model** groups matching branches and prefers a shop in your current mall.

Pickers use the same controls in shops, the catalog, the shop finder, settings, the tea lounge, the photo booth, and the material studio. Use the arrow keys or type to move to an option, Enter to choose, and Escape to close the picker. The walking controls stay below the material preview while you consult with the host.

The update adds 27 listings across seven game shops, for a total of 8,890. Five Apple stores offer iPhone 16 in three finishes, iPhone 17 in Lavender, and AirPods Pro 3. The Century City and Fashion Island TUDOR shops add the steel Black Bay 54. Game stock, prices, and purchases are simulated. Earlier purchases and saves load as before.
