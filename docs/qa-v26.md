## Limits and publication

Live UI checks use Chromium with viewport emulation, not certification on physical iPhones/iPads or Safari/Firefox. The browser-control service was intermittently unavailable. Next-day plan application and guestbook submission were completed after recovery; the 2D room fallback retains automated coverage but was not completed as a live UI flow. No claim of exhaustive browser testing is made. The character is an original animated anatomical rig; clothing, bags and room interactions remain controlled models rather than full cloth simulation or a freely walkable home. No new merchandise availability or price claims are made. See the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for remaining work and [v26 gameplay](GAMEPLAY-v26.md) for costs and rules.

Publication uses the existing public single-repository main-branch Pages workflow. Its complete checks must pass, and the public `release.json` must match the final source commit before the update is reported live.
