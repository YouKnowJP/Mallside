# Mallside v68 shopping visits

Walk up to a stocked shop and choose **Browse window**. You can also select a display window in 3D. The first two cards show the pieces in the window. Shops with more stock can show a third piece from inside.

Choose **Find in this shop** to go to the item. In 3D, the route continues inside to the exact display, even if the item is on a later shelf page. In lightweight mode, the shop opens the item’s details. Reading a window label and saving a favorite remain available from the window.

At the till, review each item and format, subtotal, game tax, total, and wallet. If payment is blocked, the till explains what needs to change. Remove an item and review the updated total before paying. The printed receipt lists each purchase and stays available in **Receipts & returns** after reload.

Warm display accents, layered window backings, clearer shelf labels, and a register with a scanner and a customer-facing terminal make it easier to tell browsing areas from checkout. The lighting uses shared baked accents within the existing scene geometry.

## Design references

- Window treatments use a quiet background and brighter merchandise accents. [ERCO shop window lighting study](https://www.erco.com/en_us/knowledge/shop/shop-window/)
- The shopping route follows a clear entry, product display, and checkout sequence. [Business Queensland retail display and customer flow](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/manufacturing-retail/retail-wholesale/retail-displays)
- Shelf labels put the game price alongside the product and its size or format. [NIST price label usability guidance](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/uniform-unit-pricing-tools-consumers-fight-shrinkflation)
