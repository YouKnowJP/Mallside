# v30 · Shared afternoons

A local story continues after its five chapters. From the next day, visit Maya, Eli, Noor, Jules or Sam to find a new **After the story** invitation below their completed chapters. The Journal’s **An afternoon with a familiar face** board routes you to the person. Each friend offers two alternating, original conversation activities, from a thank-you card and a listening club to a room palette or a newcomer’s route card.

Choose twice to shape the conversation. Each answer brings a reply and saves immediately; there are no wrong answers or required purchases. The friend recalls the direction you chose in their original story and the first choice from your last retained shared afternoon. These are small conversation scenes: imaginary cards and swatches do not become merchandise, change your wardrobe or rearrange your room.

Finishing uses the stated **10 or 12 game minutes**, grants **20 XP**, restores a little stamina and counts toward the existing one friendship bond per day. There is **one shared-memory completion per day across all five friends and all malls**. There is no direct cash reward; the usual career level-up bonus still applies if XP unlocks a level. Other daily activities keep their own limits.

Close the dialog to continue later. Your choices survive a reload, a new day or a trip; return to the original mall to finish. Only one shared activity can be underway. Leaving it clears the choices without using action time or granting a reward. Ordinary walking and travel retain their usual time costs. If closing time prevents finishing, the choices remain ready for tomorrow.

The Journal keeps the most recent **30 shared afternoons**. Open **Your shared afternoons** to reread the choices and replies; reading a memory never repeats its reward. Once the daily completion is used, all invitations wait until the next morning.

Touch targets and keyboard dialog behavior carry forward from v29. The current question receives focus and is revealed below the dialog header on short screens. Existing version-6 saves gain optional shared-visit and memory fields; completed chapters, receipts, money and the last-known-good backup system remain compatible. See [QA](qa-v30.md).
