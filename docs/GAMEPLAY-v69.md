# Mallside v69 fitting-room visits

At a stocked shop with try-on items, choose **Fitting room** from the shop controls. The tray lists the exact products and sizes already in your unpaid bag. Open **Choose from this shop** to pick something else. The fitting room takes 10 game minutes per try-on and restores your outfit when you leave the preview.

In 3D, walk into the bay, use **Turn 90°** for another angle, then choose **Done trying** to return to the shop floor. The fitting result shows which size you tried. If you picked up the same product in a different size, you can swap that bag selection to the size you tried, subject to game stock. You can also choose another size and ask the associate to check it. Payment still happens at checkout.

Stocked apparel shops have tall mirrors, warm mirror-side lighting, benches, hooks, small shelves, and paired privacy curtains. Unfinished textile shops have a furnished fabric-study area and retain their planning and material activities. Lightweight graphics show the same fitting tray and sizing choices with a simpler try-on preview.

## Design references

- The fitting bays use the mirror, seating, hook and shelf cues in the [U.S. Access Board’s fitting-room reference](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/ada-ibc-comparison/chapter-8/).
- The optional size check follows the idea of asking for help during a try-on. [SECOM’s UNIQLO fitting-room case study](https://secom.plc.uk/case-studies/smart-changing-rooms-elevating-customer-experience-at-uniqlo/).
