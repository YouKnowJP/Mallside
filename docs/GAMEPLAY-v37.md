Fourteen real models from American Eagle, BYLT Basics and SKIMS fill eleven formerly research-only shop identities. Twelve locations, including the already stocked joint Aerie/American Eagle location, receive an original collection desk and a preserved resting bench. Stocked mall windows, displays and ambient buyers now use those real collections.

The models include denim jeans and a trucker jacket, tees, a polo, shorts, pants, a ribbed tank and leggings. Names, selected colors, USD offer prices, exact source size labels and photographs were checked against the brands' official product pages on 29 October 2026. The [factual source record](research-v37/catalog-evidence.json) retains the exact URLs and selected variants. Online offers can change; these references do not assert live stock at a mall branch. Every purchase still uses simulated game money, quantities, fitting and return terms.

## Use the collection desk

Enter one of these shops. Choose **Shop floor tools & departments → Collection desk**, or interact with its physical back-wall desk. **Browse shop → Compare the collection** also opens it, including in lightweight graphics mode.

Pick two products. The desk shows each photograph, source color, fabric notes, original USD price, current game price including tax and official size labels. Long size lists can be expanded. **Review comparison** jumps to the selected pair. Open **Inspect / choose size** to read the label, fit eligible products and carry them to checkout. Comparing at this desk pauses time and spends no money or stamina; reading a label and fitting still use their displayed game time.

**Build a shopping shortlist** opens the existing budget planner. Old shortlists remain valid when a shop gains merchandise. Draft edits require an explicit save, and checkout remains at the product's named shop. The notebook and rest bench remain available; every shop retains its pinned exit.

## Construction and movement

All fourteen photographs have checksum-bound color and surface samples. Reviewed body-only sampling regions avoid model skin and unrelated clothing. Construction corrections retain short Henley sleeves, tank straps, tapered and flared pants, denim jacket pockets and the white cotton tee's actual fabric family. These are adapted game models, with the official photographs available for exact details.

Ambient shoppers keep their selected item while walking between displays, fitting rooms and checkout. Payment transfers it to a shopping bag. The carrying arm stays steadier during walking. Staff turn toward nearby shoppers, acknowledge a fresh player approach with a brief eased greeting, then return to conversation; cooldowns prevent repeated waves. Reduced-motion poses remain static.

Save keys, schema 6 and map revision 10 remain compatible. The catalog now has 1,026 stocked identities, 133 service identities and 439 pending catalogs, including 348 planning lounges and 91 original dining locations. See [release QA](qa-v37.md) and [remaining work](../ROADMAP.md).
