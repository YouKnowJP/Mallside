# Catalog expansion research · v4

Checked 24 October 2026 against official brand product pages, collection pages, brand publications and technical specifications. This expansion adds **280 listings**: fifteen named products in each of twelve new stores, plus five products in each of the original twenty stores. The integrated catalog contains **480 listings across 32 stores**, with fifteen listings per store. Listings in different stores may refer to the same actual model for comparison shopping.

## What was verified

Product names and model identities were checked against primary brand sources. Named variants such as size, material, set number, fragrance concentration and technology version were retained where the source identified them. Every product object carries its official source link. Product reference identifiers are included where directly available: LEGO set numbers, CHANEL references, selected Coach styles, Louis Vuitton codes and Hermès references.

Prices are fixed **game prices in integer US cents**, not current retail quotations. Store placement, stock, discounts, availability, fitting, return eligibility and effects are game rules. A real model appearing in Ross, Marshalls, Target or Nordstrom is not a claim that a particular branch currently sells it. Some official pages identify older, sold-out or archived models. Official sources outside the US establish product identity where the US catalog does not expose the same model. Selectable sizes are simplified game ranges, not live inventory or a fit guarantee.

Product illustrations remain simplified game art unless an independently sourced photo is supplied. No new remote photo URLs or downloaded images are included in this catalog file. No brand partnership or retailer endorsement is claimed. Fragrance and skin-care descriptions make no performance, health or ingredient claims.
