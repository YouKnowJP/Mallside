# v31 · A clearer shopping day

Explore now has a larger scene, a compact time/wallet bar and a shopping guide. The movement controls stay directly below the scene. **Menu** opens the catalog, missions, Journal, wardrobe, collection room, receipts, destinations, player options and camera/floor controls. You can return to the main menu from there too.

## Enter, shop and leave

1. Choose **Shops**, search for a name or product type, and optionally filter by floor. The default list contains stocked collections and bookable services. **All locations** also includes stores whose merchandise is still in research; those entries are marked for exploration.
2. Choose **Walk here & enter**. The route takes your shopper to the entrance, including available floor transfers. In 3D, the guide and header identify the shop you entered. The lighter 2D view opens its product list.
3. Choose **Browse shop** or a product display. Inspect a label, choose a size and try an eligible item. Adding an unpaid selection to the bag does not spend money.
4. **My bag** lists selections by shop, including size, prices, tax totals and Remove buttons. Walk to that shop’s checkout, review the total and choose **Pay**. Owned purchases and receipts remain separate from unpaid selections.
5. Choose **Exit shop** in the header or product-list footer to return to the mall. In 3D this cancels the old interior route; you will not keep walking toward its checkout after leaving. Unpaid selections remain available during the shopping day.

## Find a place and move around

The center map now has selectable storefronts and courts, names available by hover/focus, floor-connection markers and a **YOU** marker. The directory and court list provide larger button alternatives. Routing to a court from a shop first leaves the interior so the route uses the mall’s navigation graph. The marker also uses your outdoor mall position while you are inside a shop.

Held movement eases into speed and changes direction smoothly; releasing stops immediately. Camera position and its look target use time-based smoothing. A shop transition no longer counts the unrelated distance between the mall and interior as a walking stride. Stop and manual steering also cancel pending floor-transfer routes.

The gallery has additional overhead lighting and hanging planting. Physical directory panels show the current game floor and their actual location, replacing the former generic diagram. These are original game details; maps and storefronts remain adapted layouts rather than exact architectural reproductions.

## Fullscreen gameplay

**Fullscreen** fills the browser viewport with the scene and its controls, and requests native browser fullscreen when supported. The header always provides **Exit fullscreen**. If the browser declines native fullscreen, the expanded layout still fills its viewport; browser chrome remains controlled by the browser. F toggles this layout when no dialog is open. Escape closes dialogs and panels; the browser can also use Escape to leave native fullscreen.

Small screens retain Shops, Map, Browse shop while inside, My bag, Exit shop, Fullscreen and Menu. **Menu → Camera & floors** provides camera tools even when the compact bottom rail omits them. Search drawers use the available viewport height, including short landscape screens.

Save schema 6, primary/backup save keys, real-product photographs, purchase identities and earlier story rewards stay compatible. The game still has unfinished catalog research, adapted map geometry and further home-navigation work. See [remaining work](../ROADMAP.md) and [release QA](qa-v31.md).
