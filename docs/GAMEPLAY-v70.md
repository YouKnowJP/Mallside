# Mallside v70 shop staff and counters

At Irvine Spectrum’s AT&T, choose **Ask about handsets**. Riley can show the narrower bodies, compare the two 256GB selections, or point out the handset with a built-in pen. The new comparison counter has three display cradles and warm task lighting. Open a handset’s details to inspect it, or ask Riley to check the exact format. In 3D, Riley walks to that display and returns to the help point. The check leaves your bag and wallet alone until you choose to pick up or buy.

At The Game Chest in Del Amo, choose **Plan a game night**. Taylor gives different advice for two players, newcomers, and larger groups. The new table uses original card stacks and pieces. The existing material study and planning lounge are still available, and the merchandise catalog is in research. [The Game Chest Torrance](https://www.thegamechest.com/locations/torrance/).
