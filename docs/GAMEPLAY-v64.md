# Mallside v64 — the promenade update

The ten centers have distinct original paving and court details. Outdoor passages use smaller staggered stone or terracotta blocks; indoor galleries use limestone or terrazzo. Leaf geometry, tapered planters, softer fixture shadows and layered finishes help seating areas and storefronts read as physical spaces. A foreground arcade column moves out of the camera's view when it would hide the shopper, then returns as the camera moves away.

Shop windows show real items from that shop's existing catalog on recessed plinths or hangers. Inside, smaller floor joints, mineral flecks, wood grain, inset trim and a warm display-wall strip give more definition to the existing rooms. The finishes and fixtures are original game art. The [design research](research-v64/visual-direction.json) explains the real retail references that informed them.

The play header groups **Shops**, **Map**, **My bag**, **View**, fullscreen and **Menu**. Inside a shop it adds **Browse shop** and **Exit shop**. The camera and floor controls open from the walking dock. The guide stays beside the scene on wide windows and below it on smaller windows; **View** lets you hide it. **Menu** still has travel, saved progress, receipts and the option to go home.

Existing products, shop IDs, entrances, walkable floors and saves remain compatible. Payment, fitting, returns and activities retain their previous game rules. **Player, controls & saves** in the menu still offers lighter graphics and reduced motion.
