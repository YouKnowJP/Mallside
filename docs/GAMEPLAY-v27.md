# A neighborhood to come back to — v27

Five familiar faces now have **five local chapters each: 25 optional missions**, alongside the 24 main chapters and 15 friendship chapters. Maya, Eli, Noor, Jules and Sam each continue for two more days. Their original choice still matters; a completed three-chapter save opens chapter four after its saved next-day wait. Earlier rewards, receipts and memories stay collected.

Start or continue in person from **Journal → People make a place**. Actions count after beginning a chapter, at the destination where it began. Partial progress survives sleep, travel and reload. Follow the chapter for a walking route or the relevant room view. Each chapter reward is paid once.

If you completed a once-daily action before accepting a chapter, do it on a later game day. No deadline applies. Noor’s shelf step requires two kept purchases; pieces already owned work. Displaying a worn piece takes it off the shopper. Wearing it again takes it off the shelf.

## More to do at concierge

Use **Explore → Stops → Lend a little hand** to walk to the community board or lost-property desk. Both are also available from guest services. The 3D concierge has a physical noticeboard and claim tray.

- **Community notices:** keep one of three fixed invitations in the local daybook. Free, 3 game minutes and 5 XP. The invitation can route you to Eli, Jules or Noor; meeting them is optional.
- **Lost property:** read the visitor’s description and choose the matching umbrella, scarf or keyring. Free, 6 game minutes and 15 XP. A wrong answer has no cost or side effects. The claim changes with the game day. These are original activity props, never owned catalog merchandise.

Each action is rewarded once per day across all destinations, including after reload. Existing guestbook, water, wrapping, checked packages and return services remain available.

## A room you can move through

Open **My collection room**. In 3D, use **The window**, **My shelf**, **The doorway** or **The rug** to walk between furniture-safe stops. The character follows the open rug with the same dressed rig and distance-based gait used in the mall. Room movement pauses game time and does not change the mall’s saved position. Whole-room, outfit and close-up cameras remain available. Reduced motion moves directly to the chosen stop.

Open **Arrange my shelf** to move a kept purchase to its previous or next place. The physical models follow that saved order. Rearrangement does not create purchases, charge money, reopen packaging or duplicate an equipped item. The room renderer is created once per view, including when the owned-item list contains multiple products.

**Unwind** takes 8 game minutes, restores up to 12 stamina and grants 10 XP once a day. It is optional and needs enough time before closing. Prepare tomorrow’s outfit or choose **Ready to walk home & sleep** afterward. Existing work-shift pay, checked-package collection and sleep rules remain intact.

Lighter graphics retains shelf arrangement, outfits and the evening routine; the unavailable 3D movement buttons explain their disabled state. This is a bounded room with named walking stops, not unrestricted house navigation.

## Presentation and saves

Stone floors gain deterministic surface grain and different indoor/outdoor finishes. Storefront panes and entrance soffits gain layered detail without changing collision geometry. Walking adds restrained shoulder rotation, head counter-motion and bounded ankle roll during the airborne phase. Planted feet stay level; original bone lengths, held-product grips, garment constraints and reduced motion remain covered by regressions.

Save version 6 and both browser storage keys are retained. New optional daybook records normalize separately from purchases and story history. No new backend, account, photographs, catalog identities, paid services or external assets are introduced. See [release QA](qa-v27.md) and [remaining work](../ROADMAP.md).
