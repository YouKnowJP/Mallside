# Mallside v72 · Solo matches at The Game Chest

In Del Amo, find **The Game Chest** under **Shops → Collections & activities** and choose **Plan a game night**. Walk past the game shelves to Taylor’s lit table. Each game has a solo match, a guided turn, and three challenges. The library checks off the tables you win, and you can replay any of the ten.

**Play an island match** pits you against Rowan and Mina on a 19-hex island. Roll for resources, trade with the bank, and select a build action to reveal legal edges or intersections on the board. A seven lets you click a new tile for the robber. Reach ten victory points with settlements, cities, and the longest road. This table starts with placed settlements and uses a compact set of rules for solo play. [CATAN rules](https://www.catan.com/understand-catan/game-rules).

**Play a lore match** starts with a seven-card hand against Taylor. Click a card to ink or play it, select a character to quest, or click an eligible rival card to challenge or deal damage. The decks contain original Mallside cards and shuffle each time. Reach twenty lore or survive until Taylor runs out of cards. [Disney Lorcana rules](https://www.disneylorcana.com/en/resources/).

Both solo matches save after each move in your current Mallside profile on this device. Choose **Save & return to table** and then **Resume island match** or **Resume lore match**. You can also close the game and return later. The board, cards, resources and pending selections are restored. You can keep one match in progress per game. To replace it, open **Match options** and choose **Start a new match**.

Rowan and Mina now plan roads through open connections to productive settlements, look for chances to claim Longest Road, and use the robber to block rivals' strongest tiles. Taylor chooses which cards to ink and play together, can combine spell damage with a challenge, and prioritizes stopping a win on your next turn or taking a win immediately.

- **CATAN · Settlement challenge.** Trade four lumber for wool, then build on the far end of your road. The nearer intersection fails the distance rule and leaves your resources available. [Publisher rules](https://www.catan.com/understand-catan/game-rules).
- **Disney Lorcana · Quest or challenge.** You have 19 lore. Quest for the last lore immediately, or challenge an exerted opponent, see how both characters take damage, and quest after readying your character on the next turn. [Publisher tutorial](https://disneylorcana.com/en/how-to-play/).
- **CATAN · City harvest.** Upgrade one of two settlements and see how your choice affects the grain you gather on the next roll. [Publisher rules](https://www.catan.com/understand-catan/game-rules).
- **Disney Lorcana · Race to 20.** Decide whether to quest now or challenge an opponent who can win on their next turn. Once your Guide is ready, choose which of your characters quests first. [Publisher rules](https://disneylorcana.com/en/resources/).
- **CATAN · Harbor finish.** Compare the 2:1 brick harbor with the 4:1 bank trade. Keep enough brick to build at a legal site and reach ten points. [Publisher rules](https://www.catan.com/understand-catan/game-rules).
- **Disney Lorcana · Rush rescue.** Choose which card to play with two ink. Rush lets the Courier challenge while drying, and the next turn gives it a chance to quest for the winning lore. [Publisher rules](https://disneylorcana.com/en/resources/).

The challenges use original pieces and short, repeatable scenarios. You can reset them or return to the library without spending game money or time. Completed tables are saved with your existing game profile.
