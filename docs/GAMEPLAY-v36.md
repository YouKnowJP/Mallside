# v36 — Planning lounges and more natural movement

## Useful stops in unfinished shops

All 359 research-only retail locations now have an original Mallside planning lounge. Their real catalogs are still being researched. No merchant merchandise, prices or photographs have been invented for these locations.

Inside a lounge, choose **Open the shopping desk**. Browse real photographed products at their named shops in the current center. Filter by category or search by product, brand or shop. Choose a game budget of $50, $150, $300 or $1,000 and add up to three finds. The summary includes the current game price and per-item game tax, and shows when the plan exceeds the budget.

**Save shortlist** keeps the plan in the existing local save. It spends no money, advances no time, grants no rewards and reserves no stock. Draft changes are kept out of the save until explicitly saved. Saving enables a walk button for each find, taking the player into that product’s actual shop and opening its detail screen. Inspect, choose sizes, fit where eligible, and check out there. Prices can change before checkout.

Up to 16 shortlists can be kept. Open **My shortlists** from Shops or **My shopping shortlists** in the guide. Reopen, edit or remove a plan. Notes from another center offer a route to its planning lounge. Existing purchases are marked in the notebook. These notes are separate from unpaid shopping bags and the six-look outfit book.

## Furnished interiors and rest

The 3D planning lounges have original maps, budget ledgers, material studies and framed panels. Textile shops use folded materials and swatches; beauty, jewelry and eyewear locations use abstract light studies; home, book, toy and technology locations use design workbenches. These are original scenery and planning tools, not products for sale. Nearby planning stations open the relevant category, shortlist or budget section.

Each lounge has a physical bench. Use its nearby interaction or **Shop guide & options → Rest at the bench** to reach it. Resting uses the existing rules: free, 30 game minutes, up to 38 stamina recovery, with ordinary hunger passing. The bench, tables and partitions keep their reserved collision footprints and every shop keeps its persistent exit control.

Public benches gain inset cushions and seam detail; small planters gain gravel detail. These additions fit existing furniture footprints.

## Character and crowd refinements

3D ambient shoppers start at spaced, walkable points outside the player’s immediate space. Compact courts can use another court when their local space is full. Small side steps and slower approaches reduce walking overlap and abrupt arrivals. Existing reduced-motion and crowd preferences remain available.

Head turns ease between attention targets. Sitting and standing use a smooth curve, and seated placement keeps knees in front of bench edges. Studio and nearby seated visitors use the same placement rule. Manual player input still stops immediately on release.

Save keys and schema 6 remain compatible. Optional shortlists default empty and are bounded and validated on import. See [release QA](qa-v36.md) and [remaining work](../ROADMAP.md).
