# Local stories, guest services and atmosphere — v25

Open **Journal → People make a place**, or meet Maya, Eli and Sam through **Explore → Stops**. Meet a regular, choose a direction, then follow the chapter above the mall. These nine optional chapters are separate from the 24 main story chapters and nine friendship chapters.

## Three stories, six paths

| Regular | Choice | Three-chapter arc |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maya | Something useful / A little personal style | Read labels and listen; buy and wrap a real gift; return for a photo, rest and catch-up. |
| Eli | The quieter corners / A little more playful | Plan a route and take a first detour; arrange an art-and-flowers or ride-and-cinema afternoon; make a photo memory and catch up. |
| Sam | A practical loop / The scenic route | Learn the directory and water stop; take a reading-and-rest or terrace-tea afternoon; share a three-shop route. |

Completed actions **after beginning a chapter** count. A previously discovered product has an explicit **Read label again** action with the same three-minute/two-stamina cost; it gives no additional discovery XP. Distinct-label tasks compare named models, so repeated branches do not multiply a label. Visit, label, conversation and activity evidence belongs to the mall where that chapter began. The chapter card names that mall; following the chapter returns to it. There is no deadline. An unfinished chapter survives sleep, travel and reload. Claiming a chapter opens its next chapter on the following game day; each story takes at least three days. Choices remain fixed for that story, and rewards can be collected once per chapter.

One optional objective is tracked at a time. Choosing a local chapter switches away from the daily outing. Tracking an outing or the main story switches back without cancelling either plan. Hunger, stamina and closing guidance still take priority.

## Maya's parcel

The second chapter accepts a new purchase made after that chapter begins, with its actual receipt total at most $150 including game tax. The useful path accepts nonwearable home, beauty, technology and hobby models; the style path accepts wearables/accessories in the player's usual size. Food and drinks are excluded. Read the exact purchased listing's label and keep the item unused with tags intact. Qualifying purchases can come from any unlocked mall.

Wrap the item at guest services, then meet Maya and explicitly select **Give to Maya**. The purchase leaves the player's inventory and cannot be returned or used. Maya reimburses the receipt total and its $2 wrapping fee, and pays the chapter reward. Returned, used, checked or previously delivered items cannot qualify. Older purchases and unpaid bags do not qualify.

## Guest services

- **Gift wrapping:** choose sage, rose or midnight paper for a real unused purchase. Costs $2 game money and six minutes. Tags remain intact. Wrap a purchase once; unwrap it free in one minute before wearing, using or returning. The wrapping fee itself is not refunded. Collect checked packages first. A short two-hand wrapping animation sets handhelds aside, then restores them, and shows a parcel; the paper does not change the actual product identity or construction.
- **Water station:** a free cup, or a refill when the player already has an opened usable bottle. Three minutes and eight stamina recovery; it does not replace a meal. A 30-minute game cooldown prevents repeated immediate use.
- **Directory notes:** at a physical directory, choose **Save a route note**. This uses three minutes and contributes to the relevant local chapter. Reading the map alone remains paused and free of time cost.

Water and wrapping have physical counter details in the 3D guest-services scene. Existing package check, receipts and return policies remain available.

## Visual changes

Player walking now follows the actual continuous travel heading instead of only four cardinal angles. Player and NPC turns use elapsed-time smoothing. Conversation routes stop beside a person; ambient shoppers yield near the player and keep moving during activities. The same rig dimensions, planted-foot solving, garment fit and bag attachment rules remain. Browsing adds small bounded head attention; reduced motion restores a neutral head. Ambient adults use six deterministic skin/hair/face combinations without increasing crowd counts.

Existing lights and sky/fog colors transition from morning to afternoon, golden hour and evening. Indoor light remains readable. Window illumination changes without adding per-store lights. Instanced storefront sills, frame details, thresholds and warm display strips add depth. Benches gain slat detail, and planted foliage has smoother silhouettes. These are original adapted scenes, not exact architectural replicas or full physical cloth simulation.

Optional fields extend save version 6. Invalid local-story or wrapping data is normalized independently of money and ownership. No new merchandise, product prices, photographs, external assets, dependencies, payments or network save services were added.
