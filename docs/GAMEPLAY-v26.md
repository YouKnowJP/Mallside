Open **Journal → People make a place** to meet Noor and Jules alongside Maya, Eli and Sam. Five original local stories now offer **15 optional chapters**, with two choices for each story. The original nine chapters and completed rewards remain intact. Each regular also has three friendship chapters, bringing that separate system to 15 chapters.

Begin a chapter in person. Actions completed afterward count at the mall where it began. Progress survives sleep, travel and reload; the next chapter opens the following game day. The first choice stays fixed. No deadline applies, and each reward is paid once. Existing owned purchases can satisfy Noor’s wardrobe steps; another purchase is optional.

Newly saving a look or newly placing a purchase counts toward an active chapter. An already displayed purchase can be put away and chosen again. If today’s outfit or guestbook page was completed before beginning a chapter, complete that action on a later day. Old actions are not replayed as new evidence.

## Your collection room

Use **Main menu → My collection room** or the wardrobe’s room link. The furnished 3D planning view uses the same anatomical shopper, equipped real products, fit rules and materials as the mall. Kept purchases appear as physical models in the six shelf compartments. Choose Garden studio, Warm oak or Evening blue; the finish persists in the save.

Use **Whole room**, **My outfit** or **Closer look**, then **Stand**, **Take a seat** or **Walk in place** to inspect the character. The lighter graphics setting retains the illustrated preview and all planning actions. Shelf arrangement and collection goals are collapsible to keep the main room view clear.

Only opened or worn purchases can be displayed. Opening or wearing an item ends its game return eligibility; the existing confirmation and receipt rules still apply. Checked purchases must be collected first. Placing a worn piece on the shelf takes it off the shopper; wearing it or applying a saved/prepared look brings it back from the shelf. Opening another item for display preserves the rest of the current outfit. The room is a planning view; it does not duplicate purchases or move them into a second inventory.

## Prepare tomorrow’s outfit

1. Equip at least two kept purchases in different slots, in your saved usual sizes.
2. In the room, select **Prepare my current outfit · 5 min**.
3. After sleeping, use **Put on today’s prepared look**.

Preparation uses five game minutes and one stamina plus the ordinary time-based need changes. One plan can be prepared each day. It grants no direct money or XP and opens no packaging. The selected purchase IDs survive reload and sleep. Applying a plan is free; every piece must still be owned, available, opened and in the current saved size. A missing or checked piece produces a clear message without partially changing equipment. Older plans remain memories; only today’s plan can be applied.

## The concierge guestbook

Walk to guest services and choose **Guestbook**. Pick a mood and one highlight, then confirm. Closing before confirmation costs nothing. Signing uses five game minutes and awards 10 XP once per game day across all malls. It is an original Mallside activity, inspired by the role of central guest services in a shopping center.

The character uses a two-hand writing pose with a small notepad; held merchandise is set aside for the animation and restored afterward. A saved page appears in the room’s guestbook memories. Entries contain fixed mood/highlight choices and location/day data, with no public posting or network save service.

## A more lived-in center

Ambient shoppers pause at actual storefronts, turn toward the display and resume their route. Their gait advances by actual movement, including when yielding. Regulars turn toward the player during a held conversation. Starter tops use shared skinned sleeve material groups and a collar; purchased tops replace those layers. Small seated hand motion respects held bags and reduced motion. Stone, timber, glass, foliage and lighting use distinct, bounded surface responses while retaining instanced architecture.
