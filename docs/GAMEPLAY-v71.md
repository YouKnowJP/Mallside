# Mallside v71 Game Chest practice table

Visit The Game Chest on Del Amo’s upper level and choose **Plan a game night**. Taylor recommends Disney Lorcana for two players or a first example turn, and explains how to seat a group of three to six for CATAN. Both demos are available from any answer. [Del Amo directory](https://www.simon.com/mall/del-amo-fashion-center/stores/print).

- **CATAN** uses the sixth-edition base rules. Roll a fixed 8, collect lumber, then spend it with a brick to build beside your settlement. Trying the disconnected edge explains the rule and leaves your resources available for another attempt. Catan Studio lists the Torrance branch in its Return to Catan demo program. [CATAN rules](https://www.catan.com/understand-catan/game-rules) · [Catan Studio locator](https://www.catanstudio.com/return-to-catan-store-locator/).
- **Disney Lorcana** lets you ink a card, play a character, wait for its ink to dry, and quest for one lore. Ravensburger records a sealed *Attack of the Vine* prerelease at the Torrance branch on 18 July 2026, local time. [Lorcana rules](https://disneylorcana.com/en/how-to-play/) · [Prerelease record](https://tcg.ravensburgerplay.com/events/712268).

The practice turns use original Mallside illustrations and generic pieces. You can reset either turn or go back to Taylor’s selected advice. Practice is free and pauses the mall clock. The publisher records establish branch activity. Current inventory and prices are unverified.
