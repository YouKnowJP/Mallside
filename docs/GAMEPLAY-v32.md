# v32 — More life between the shops

Every one of the 23 public floor views now has three original Mallside discoveries, spread between its courts:

- **Mosaic table:** turn three tiles clockwise until their arrows match ↑ → ↓, then save the arrangement.
- **Reading shelf:** choose one of three original micro-stories and remember a page.
- **Garden study:** choose a detail of the pocket garden to sketch in your notebook.

Open **Map → Things to do on this floor** to walk to a stop. Purple stars on the diagram are also interactive. Benches, kiosks and directories have their own expandable list. Blue floor arrows now lead to the floor chooser. Routes work from inside shops and across floors.

Each activity is free and takes four or five game minutes, restoring a little stamina. The first discovery at each stop grants 5 XP. Discover all three kinds in a mall for a one-time 25 XP trail bonus. You can read the material freely afterward; a new note and stamina recovery are available the following game day. **My discoveries** opens the notebook, which survives reload, save export and import. Each mall keeps its own trail.

The additions are original game public spaces. They do not describe actual center amenities or certify exact real-world geometry. Compact street and upper courts have more room for activities and a resting spot. Stone inlays, book displays, planted tables and shaded outdoor benches add physical detail in both the 3D scene and illustrated fallback.

## Shop options

- A saved bag’s checkout action walks to that shop and continues to its counter automatically. Review the total and press Pay to purchase.
- Service shops explain reservations, use and unused-booking refunds, with a visible Exit button and consistent counter labels. Refunds require the original mall. Cinema sessions stay inside the service interior.
- Nonwearable and child-size collections use demos and browsing rather than an empty fitting studio. Associates offer fitting only for eligible collections.
- The full directory distinguishes stocked products, bookable services and collections still in research.
- Collapsible dialog sections are included in keyboard navigation; closed section contents cannot trap focus.

Existing save keys and the version-6 save format remain compatible. Legacy saves start with an empty discovery notebook; purchases, wallets, stories and recovery backups retain their existing behavior.
