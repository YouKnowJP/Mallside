# v41 — Working studios and courtyard mosaics

All 327 remaining planning shops gain an original material-study desk, with three named palettes and textured/smooth finishes. Textile studios have folded and stitched swatches; light studios use domed samples and inset panels; workshops use small joinery models. Slatted table aprons, handles, foot rails, notebooks and three-part wall galleries add construction detail within existing fixture footprints.

Use **Shops → Material studios** to find these locations. Open **Material study** from the shop guide or floor tools, or walk to its physical desk/nook. Palette and finish controls update the actual 3D samples. Save the study for this shop, or close/leave to discard the preview. Saved choices reappear on later visits and reloads. The player guide explains the studio and shopping desk separately; proximity-based interaction signs and a smaller wall sign keep the room detail visible. The screen links to the existing verified-product planner and keeps a direct shop exit visible on small screens. Saving is free, consumes no game time, awards no XP and does not reserve or sell anything.

These are original Mallside creative props. Pending catalogs stay pending; no merchant products, real inventory or branch interiors are invented. The catalog remains at 8,730 listings, with every earlier product ID intact.

All 23 courtyard mosaic tables gain inset ceramic chips, grout, a border and three physical rotating arrow tiles. Native keyboard and touch controls turn the actual table, matching the activity screen. Closing without saving restores the previous arrangement; saving and reloading retain the aligned route on its mall floor. The existing five-minute discovery cost, XP, stamina and repeat limits remain unchanged. The 2D court draws the same current arrangement.

Fitting curtains now use elapsed-time easing for their opening width and position, retain their pose while paused, and snap under reduced motion. Leaving a room clears its procedural finish cache so later rooms track and release their own textures.

Schema-6 saves and map revision 10 remain compatible. `materialStudies` is optional, validated against actual planning shops, deduplicated per shop and bounded by those shops. Older saves receive an empty notebook; malformed optional entries cannot replace purchases or other progress.
