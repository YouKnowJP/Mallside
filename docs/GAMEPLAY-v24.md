# Daily outings and branch-aware progress — v24

From the main menu, choose **Plan today’s outing**, or open **Journal** during a game. Career and free shop retain separate saves and outings. The planner pauses the live clock.

## Choose a kind of afternoon

- **One good find:** inspect an available wearable model, try your usual size and keep a new purchase within the outing allowance.
- **An outfit, together:** inspect a top and a bag/hat/belt, fit the top and keep both within one allowance.
- **The little gift hunt:** inspect a nonwearable real model and keep a new purchase to bring home. This is a shopping brief, not a claim that gift wrapping or NPC delivery is implemented.
- **A window-shopping afternoon:** read three different real-model labels today, then take a new free bench rest. No purchase is necessary.

Offers use existing active catalog products and their sourced photographs. They are deterministic for that day, mall, level, available usual-size stock, wallet and planning target. Models rotate across days. Unaffordable paid outings are omitted; the free outing remains available. Prices and stock are simulated game rules, not live retailer inventory.

## Follow the checklist

Accepting an outing reserves no money. Its allowance includes the selected finds and game tax; meals, activities and unrelated purchases are separate. Lowering the general daily budget does not change an already accepted allowance. Offer estimates exclude a one-use coupon and show a minimum action duration; walking and queues add time.

**Continue this outing** tracks its next incomplete step in the existing objective pill above the mall. **Track my story instead** returns to chapter guidance. Item buttons route to the shops used in the plan, preserving their quoted price context. Matching named models at other verified branches can count too, provided the paid total remains within the allowance.

Inspection/fit steps use today’s evidence. Purchase steps require actual new paid receipts after acceptance, not an unpaid bag or an older owned item. A fitting-based goal also requires the usual purchased size. Returned or delivered items stop counting before reward collection. Keeping tags intact allows the normal return process. An unused return refunds original tax as before.

Collect the reward explicitly when every step is complete. Only one outing reward is paid per day, even after cancellation, reload or backup import. Cancellation preserves purchases, wallet and story progress; a replacement paid outing requires purchases made after accepting that replacement. Outings expire overnight without a penalty. The morning recap records progress, completed outing memories remain in the planner, and new offers arrive the next day.

## Story and request fixes

Named-retailer goals derive identity from the actual store’s normalized merchant name. An UNIQLO purchase at Brea can now satisfy an UNIQLO story goal, and a Round Mini Shoulder Bag bought at a verified branch satisfies the same named-model requirement. Nordstrom Rack remains distinct from Nordstrom. Passport stamps count the required story retailers once. Existing raw shop IDs, receipts, stock, visits, earned chapters and claimed rewards are retained.

Different-model counts use normalized brand plus the full product name, matching the game’s model-level comparisons. Repeated listings or source IDs of the same named model do not become additional models. This is not a manufacturer SKU/variant equivalence guarantee. Destination discovery filters to that destination’s exact store IDs before model deduplication, so inspecting a retailer somewhere else cannot complete a mall’s passport. Geographic districts, postcards and location-specific activities retain their location requirements.

Store-restricted community requests accept eligible branches while retaining the original budget, exact purchased-listing inspection, correct size, purchase-after-acceptance, same-day, unused, unreturned, undelivered and unchecked requirements.

## Compatibility and limits

Saved-state version 6 gains optional outing fields; prior saves get safe defaults without resetting money, ownership, progression, appearance or claims. Malformed outing records are discarded independently. No new real-product prices or photographs were invented for this release.

The game still uses adapted mall geometry and controlled character/fabric animation. This release does not make every unresearched tenant catalog complete or certify every browser/device. See [remaining work](../ROADMAP.md) and [QA](qa-v24.md).
