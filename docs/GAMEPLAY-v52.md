# v52 · A room you can use

The collection room now supports free walking across its open floor. Tap or click the floor to choose a route; focus the room and use arrows or WASD for direct movement. Directions follow the screen. Tap a touch-pad direction to step or hold it to walk. Escape and Stop cancel movement. Furniture, walls and room edges block walking, and routes begin at the character's current position.

The six named stops remain optional shortcuts. Clicking a furnished station walks there and uses it on arrival. E or the nearby action button uses a station within reach. Equivalent room shortcuts work without walking, including in lighter graphics mode:

- The shelf opens its existing six-place arrangement controls. Moving and putting away owned finds preserves the original ownership and return rules.
- The wardrobe opens kept purchases, named looks and the dressing room.
- The memory desk shows recent activity and guestbook memories, with links to portraits and stories. Reading is free.
- The window offers the existing daily evening pause, with its stated eight-minute cost, stamina recovery and once-per-day reward.
- The doorway returns directly to the current mall and floor.

A new original wardrobe, layered notebooks, shelf labels, woven curtain folds and a locally generated garden view make the room fuller. Beveled furniture, timber grain and rug texture refine surfaces. These are original decorative game props, not additional merchant inventory. Existing six-slot displays still show the purchases you own.

Direct keyboard and held-pad movement eases into its walking speed, follows actual distance for gait, and stops on release. Leaving the canvas, hiding the window or opening a dialog clears movement and pending station actions. Each studio removes its input listeners when destroyed. The touch pad keeps its position when status text changes, so releasing a direction cannot retarget the click onto Stop. A seat returns to the clear rug before sitting; it does not appear inside furniture. Reduced motion keeps safe destinations and stationary poses. Camera and pose buttons reflect the actual studio state.

Room movement and reading do not spend money, advance time or award XP. The existing evening pause retains its original cost and reward. Room positions are temporary; shelf order, room finish, ownership, prepared outfits and memories retain their existing saves. Save schema 6, map revision 10 and the full 8,847-product catalog remain compatible. No new merchant offers or product photographs are introduced in this update.

More rooms, unrestricted furniture placement, physical mobile hardware and broader screen-reader testing remain on the roadmap. See [release QA](qa-v52.md) for tested browsers and practical limits.
