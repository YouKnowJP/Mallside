# v29 · Touch and keyboard access

The walking controls have their own area below the mall map. On short landscape screens they sit beside it. Tap a direction for a small step, or hold it to keep walking. Release, move off the button or choose **Stop** to stop. Stop also clears a chosen walking route. Paused games and open panels do not queue walking inputs.

Touch players can still tap an open floor tile to choose a route or drag on the canvas to steer. A second finger, cancelled gesture or lost capture cannot leave the character drifting. Opening a dialog, changing focus or hiding the page clears held movement. Shops and Stops provide button-based alternatives to steering.

**Map & camera** contains floor selection, zoom, camera rotation and fullscreen. Collapsing these tools leaves more space for play. Directories and interiors receive extra height on narrow screens, and the controls have at least 44px core touch targets. The clock display is a pause/resume button with its current state announced.

Use Tab to reach the skip link, navigation, map and controls. With the map focused, arrows or WASD walk and E uses the nearest stop. Tab leaves the canvas and stops held movement. Enter or Space can operate a direction button; brief presses step and holds walk. Browser shortcuts such as Ctrl/Command-F retain their normal behavior. F without a modifier still toggles expanded play.

Escape closes a dialog or map drawer and returns focus, or collapses focused map/camera tools. Outside those panels, Escape opens the main menu. Dialogs cycle forward and backward through visible enabled controls and make the game background inactive. Navigation identifies the current page.

Player options retain larger text, reduced motion and lighter 2D graphics. Text colors now have stronger contrast; achievements say whether they are earned. Short screens and larger text may require normal vertical scrolling. Browser/page zoom remains available; no maximum-scale restriction was added.

Progress uses the same version-6 saves and last-known-good backups. Movement controls do not charge money or change ownership. The release does not claim full WCAG conformance or physical iOS/Android certification; see [the test scope](qa-v29.md).
