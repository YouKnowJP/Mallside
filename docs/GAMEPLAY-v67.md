# Mallside v67 play tables and workbenches

Del Amo’s Toys R Us at Macy’s now has a staffed toy shop with a play table, stocked displays, and a bench. Browse Jenga Classic, the original 3×3 Rubik’s Cube, and Scrabble Classic. Choose **Explore the play table** in the shop or walk to the table in 3D.

- Slide, lift, and set a Jenga block on top of the tower.
- Turn the top layer of the cube in either direction and watch the adjacent colors change.
- Place letter tiles in order to build MALL and see its score before board bonuses.

The samples are free and pause the clock. Buy an item through the usual bag and checkout, or return to the shop floor when you’re done.

Verizon at Irvine Spectrum now has a Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra in Titanium Silverblue with 256 GB. Its display shows the front and rear, and the two-handed inspection shows the separate camera rings and S Pen cap.

Planning lounges have more detailed workbenches. The host gestures when you change the palette or finish and settles back into a relaxed stance. Reduced motion keeps the preview stationary. Earlier shopping plans, material notes, and purchases remain compatible.
