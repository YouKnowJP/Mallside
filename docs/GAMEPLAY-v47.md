# v47 — collectible studios and mosaic courts

Four unfinished Hot Topic shops—Brea, The Outlets at Orange, Del Amo and Irvine Spectrum—gain four photographed Sanrio Japan Originals plush references: Hello Kitty, Cinnamoroll, Kuromi and My Melody. Official Hot Topic US pages and exact photographs were checked on 31 October 2026. Hello Kitty uses the dated automatic $27.92 sale reference, reduced from $34.90. The other listed references exclude Hot Cash and promotional-code reductions; My Melody showed low shipping stock. These are online references, not promises about branch inventory, real return rights or photo licensing.

The one unsized format does not imply clothing fit. Cinnamoroll's page lists approximately six inches without an axis; the other pages publish no dimensions. Solid models use reviewed colors, ears, face details and clothing/hood construction as original adapted game art, with the exact source photograph available for comparison.

The original collector studios add comparison trays, seam samples and a retained free material study. Pick up a collectible, put it back, inspect it in a two-hand animation, compare the actual source references, buy it, or return an unopened purchase for its exact game refund. Existing six-minute demonstrations keep their normal once-per-listing reward. Opening a purchased collectible uses the existing ten-minute rule and removes tags; opened items can no longer be returned. The collection room can display the new solid models through its existing shelf controls.

All 23 mosaic courts gain inset joinery, spare tiles, notebook detail and a marker for the actual floor's saved discovery. The nearby discovery-notebook button is free to read and changes no money, clock or XP. Tile turns now use a finite 360ms eased transition that reaches its endpoint at different refresh rates; reduced motion snaps directly. Batched details reduce each mosaic study from 73 separate mesh draws to five drawables, while preserving the existing collision footprint, puzzle, cancellation, save and reward rules.

All 8,806 earlier product records and appearances remain compatible. The catalog now has 8,822 branch listings, 1,071 stocked identities and 394 catalogs awaiting research, including 303 retail planning shops. Schema 6 and map revision 10 stay unchanged.
