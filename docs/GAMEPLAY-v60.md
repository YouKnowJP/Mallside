## Purchased parcels, clearly separated from unpaid picks

Guest services now opens with **carried / 8** and **checked** counts. It identifies each checked purchase by product, selected format, original shop and purchase day, and provides direct collection and receipt-review controls. Checking today’s carried purchases remains free and takes five game minutes; each collection takes three. A full carrying allowance disables collection and explains how to free space. Unpaid shop selections remain in their existing bags and cannot be deposited as purchased parcels.

All 23 existing counters gain up to three physical parcel markers, while the guide always shows the exact count. Details remain on the existing counter footprints and update without allocating per-frame geometry. Shared concierge storage across centers and overnight delivery home are existing game rules, not representations of real mall services. Existing tags, eligibility, original-shop returns, tax-inclusive refunds, ownership and saved receipt IDs retain their rules.

Associates now start return walks gently after a stationary conversation or display check. Cancelling while already walking preserves current speed and position. Paused and reduced-motion behavior remains usable.
