# v33 — a more polished shopping scene

The mall and collection room now use filtered environment lighting, softer grounding shadows and more distinct stone, timber, fabric, glass, metal and water finishes. Beveled architectural details, layered roof tiles, finer palm fronds and fountain ripples add detail without requiring an asset download. Starter clothing has a proper shirt shell and sleeves, and character close-ups have cleaner collars and brows.

Choose **View** in the play header or game menu:

- **Closer view** frames people, shopfronts and the route ahead. **Raised view** gives a wider perspective. Both retain zoom and rotation controls.
- **Hide shopping guide** gives the scene more room. Show it again through View. Compact fullscreen already uses the available space for play.
- **Show shopper activity captions** restores incidental browsing captions when wanted. The default keeps the scene quieter.

Camera, guide and caption choices persist in the existing save. They do not alter purchases, money, inventory, missions or backups. The illustrated fallback retains its own zoom/overview controls.

Selecting a map destination adds visible markers along the real walking route. **Stop** clears the route and its markers. Shop entrances retain explicit Browse, Bag and Exit actions; the closer indoor camera keeps the player visible and shows the mall threshold outside the shop.
