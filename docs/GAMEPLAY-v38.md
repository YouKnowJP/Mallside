# v38 — Bookshops and reading corners

Eight verified physical editions bring stock to all six previously research-only Barnes & Noble identities. They include paperback and hardcover editions of The Art Thief, plus The Creative Act, Piranesi, The Wild Robot, A Psalm for the Wild-Built, Braiding Sweetgrass and The Art of DreamWorks The Wild Robot.

Each listing records its exact ISBN-13, physical format, publisher, author, dated USD offer and matching cover. The [edition evidence](research-v38/book-evidence.json) comes from the retailer's selected physical variants. Member discounts, ebooks and audiobook trials are not substituted for physical-edition prices. Real branch inventory is not asserted; quantities, fitting, taxes and returns remain simulated game rules. Photographs retain their independent ownership and existing rights notices.

## Explore the shop

The new shops have solid book models with matching covers, page blocks, boards and spines. Original edition desks, book stacks, bookends, lamps and preserved rest benches fill the former planning lounges. Existing fixture footprints and collision-free approaches remain in place. These interiors are original condensed game spaces, not claims of exact branch architecture.

Choose **Compare the collection** or visit the physical collection desk. Compare two editions, check format, price, ISBN and publication notes, then open **Cover & edition**. The product screen labels the choice as **EDITION** and offers **Browse edition** alongside Add to bag. It does not offer clothing fitting for books.

Browsing uses six game minutes and two stamina, checks the edition facts and plays a reading/page-turning moment. The animated inside pages are original Mallside props; full book text is not included. Finish the moment at any time. Browsing preserves purchase tags and does not spend money or reserve stock. Preview XP is awarded once per ISBN across branches; repeat browsing still uses its displayed time and stamina.

Pay at the actual shop's checkout. Unworn, tagged books keep their ordinary game return eligibility. Paperback and hardcover receipts retain their separate edition identities and original paid tax.

## Connect the public reading corners

All 23 public reading shelves gain inset paper/bookmark and bookend details within their existing footprints. Their original micro-stories and discovery rewards remain unchanged.

**Reading shelf → Find verified bookshops** lists stocked book and stationery shops in the current center first. Other centers show their floor and rank requirement. Walk buttons use the existing physical shop/travel routes. Research-only shops remain planning lounges and are not presented as stocked bookshops.

## Movement and compatibility

Reading adds bounded hand motions, eased page turns and a gentle downward glance, with hand and page timing kept together. Existing semantic pose easing smooths the transition back to standing. Reduced motion keeps the reading pose and page still. The temporary reading prop releases its resources and restores held items when the moment finishes.

Existing saves, receipts, wishes, demos and shortlists remain compatible under save schema 6 and map revision 10. Earlier bookshop shortlists still open after the shop gains stock.
