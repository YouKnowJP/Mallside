# v35 — Dining rooms and quiet discoveries

The real catalogs remain evidence-based. This update adds original Mallside activities to existing places, with no new merchant products or changes to real item identities.

## Food locations

All 249 food branches now offer a clearly labeled original game dining menu. This includes 91 locations whose real product collections are still being researched. Cafés, dessert stops and restaurants share appropriate selections from the existing Mallside meals. Prices, time, fullness and stamina are shown before ordering; they are game values, not the venue’s actual menu or prices.

In a 3D dining room, open **Browse shop** or **Dining menu**, review a meal, then choose **Walk to a seat & dine**. Money is charged only when the route arrives. Stop or exit before arrival to cancel. The lightweight 2D mode routes to the branch door before serving its meal. The last 120 dining visits persist in the existing save; a location’s menu lists its three most recent breaks.

Research-only food rooms have six furnished tables with chairs, place settings and planting. The table footprints block walking correctly. Real-product collections remain available where researched; **Collection guide** and **Receipts & returns** remain separate options. Every shop keeps its pinned exit button.

## Sculpture alcoves

Each of the 23 public floors has a reachable **Sculpture alcove** in a quieter court. Find its purple map marker or named public-stop button. The original Two Lines sculpture offers three observations: curves, shadows and patina. Save one sketch per floor per day for three game minutes and four stamina recovery; only the first sketch on that floor grants five XP. The sketchbook remembers all visited floors without changing the original three-stop discovery trail.

Outdoor ground floors also have a landscaped base beneath the walkways, so the site reads as a connected place. The base is scenery; the established walkable areas and building boundaries still control movement.

## Shop and animation fixes

- Old purchases at research-only shops reach the actual returns desk, retaining the original receipt, tag, branch and time-window checks.
- Original collection-guide furniture has collision footprints matching its visible tables. Shops without product stock no longer have invisible stock fixtures.
- Character arms ease between semantic gestures over 0.28 seconds; reduced motion remains immediate. Static hand poses reuse their solved joint transforms.
- Nearby seated visitors retain their seats when the player crosses an ambient-region boundary.
- Shop lists and directory labels distinguish game dining from researched product collections.

Save keys and schema 6 stay compatible. Dining and sketchbook fields default empty in old saves and are validated on import. See [release QA](qa-v35.md) and [remaining development](../ROADMAP.md).
