# Mallside v65 — dining rooms and food halls

Explore an unfinished dining location through **Shops** or the center map. Look for laid tables, tray displays and a two-cushion bench. **Browse shop** opens the illustrated game menu. Review a meal, choose the left or right seat in 3D, then start walking. Stop or exit the shop before you reach the seat to cancel. The 2D game serves the same meal at the branch entrance.

Use **Stops** or **Map** to visit a public food hall. Review a meal for its game time, fullness, stamina and price. Go back to compare the other options or confirm the meal. Water is free. Short on game money or time before closing? The review explains why the meal is unavailable.

The seated shopper settles before lifting a mug, snack, sandwich, spoon or cone, then lowers it before standing. Reduced motion shows a stable pose. The menus, illustrations and furnishings are original game content. Restaurant catalog research remains pending.
