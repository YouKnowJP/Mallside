# v53 · Fragrance studio and useful rest corners

Guerlain at South Coast Plaza moves from a research lounge to a stocked collection. It offers three exact selected references: Aqua Allegoria Bergamote Calabria Eau de Toilette, 125 mL spray, SKU G014385 ($170); Mandarine Basilic Eau de Toilette, 125 mL spray, SKU G014395 ($170); and Nerolia Vetiver Eau de Toilette, 75 mL spray, SKU G014413 ($125). These are official US online offers checked 4 November 2026. Other sizes, refills and gift sets are excluded; the references do not establish current branch inventory.

The original studio adds plaster niches, fluted woodwork, brass edging, bottle displays, paper strips and a source comparison desk. A free fragrance notebook presents each reference’s published notes side by side and links to its exact product card. Reading and comparing pause time and spend no money, stamina or XP. Existing material studies and shopping shortlists remain available, including notes saved before this collection was stocked. Checkout, eligible returns, resting and the direct exit retain their existing rules.

Solid original glass models use transparent shells, colored reservoirs, rounded shoulders, gold ribs, collars, closed caps and plain original labels. Source photographs establish the selected identities and appearances; bottle dimensions are adapted and are not claimed to be merchant measurements. The game does not copy the merchant’s label artwork onto its models. Inspection lifts the bottle, turns it gently while both hands follow the same targets, then lowers it. Reduced motion holds a fixed pose. Spraying, refilling, scent, longevity and skin suitability are not simulated.

Owned opened bottles can be arranged on the existing six-place collection shelf. Opening takes the original ten game minutes and removes return eligibility. The six-minute shop inspection does not consume a purchase, and repeating a demonstration cannot earn duplicate discovery XP.

All 23 public rest lounges gain original slatted joinery, arms, planted pots, a folded floor guide and paper details. Narrow footprints receive a correspondingly slimmer bench; the additions remain inside the existing venue boundaries. The lounge card keeps its original free 30-minute rest and up-to-38-stamina recovery, and now links to saved shortlists, accessible stocked shops on the current floor, the map and lunch. Reading the guide is free. Shop visits use actual walking and existing floor connections. Separate upper wings still use their established escalator/bridge route rather than an invented same-floor corridor.

Sitting and standing ease their acceleration at both ends of the existing animation. Starting a rest turns toward the seat smoothly instead of snapping to its direction. Keyboard navigation, lighter graphics and direct exits retain equivalent actions.

All 8,847 earlier complete product records and every shop identity remain compatible. There are now 8,850 product listings, 1,079 stocked identities, 133 service identities and 386 identities awaiting catalog research; 295 retail research lounges remain.
