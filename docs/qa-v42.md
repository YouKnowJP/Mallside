## Limits

Desktop native browsers and touch emulation do not certify physical mobile devices or full manual screen-reader use. Furnishings are original Mallside scenes; game stock, fit and prices are simulated. Exact branch geometry, wider catalog research, broader character/clothing work and long-session GPU stress remain unfinished.
